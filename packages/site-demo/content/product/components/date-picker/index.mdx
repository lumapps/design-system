## Input

`DatePickerField` is a [text field](/product/components/text-field) where users can type a date or pick one in a calendar.
A click on the input (or <kbd>↓</kbd>) opens the calendar in a dialog.

<DemoBlock orientation="horizontal" demo={{ react: ReactDemoInput, vue: VueDemoInput }} />

### Typed dates

Users can type a date in the input. The field reads the text on <kbd>Enter</kbd> and when the input loses focus,
then shows the selected date again in the long format of the `locale` (e.g. "November 12, 2026").
The field accepts:

-   The long format of the `locale`, with the month names of the `locale` (e.g. "November 12, 2026", "12 novembre 2026").
-   A numeric date, in the day, month and year order of the `locale` (e.g. `11/12/2026` in `en-US`,
    `12/11/2026` in `fr-FR`). Any non-digit characters separate the parts (`12.11.2026`, `12-11-2026`).
-   A two-digit year: the nearest year, from 50 years back to 49 years forward (`85` is 1985, `26` is 2026).
-   A day and a month with no year: the current year (e.g. `11/12` in `en-US`).

A date outside of `minDate` and `maxDate` is moved to the nearest of the two.
Any other text (a single number, an impossible date like `02/31/2026`) is ignored: the input shows the selected date again.

**A date field can become empty only if it has a clear button** (`clearButtonProps`). Without it, an empty
text is ignored like an invalid text.

### Keyboard

On the input:

-   <kbd>↓</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>↓</kbd> opens the calendar and focuses the selected day (else today).
-   <kbd>Enter</kbd> reads the typed date. It does not open the calendar.
-   <kbd>Space</kbd> and the other characters type in the input.

In the calendar dialog:

-   <kbd>Tab</kbd> moves between the previous month button, the year input, the next month button and the calendar grid (one
    tab stop, on the selected day).
-   <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> move to the previous and next day, <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to the same day of the
    previous and next week. They cross into the previous and next month.
-   <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> move to the first and last day of the week.
-   <kbd>Page Up</kbd> and <kbd>Page Down</kbd> move to the same day of the previous and next month, with
    <kbd>Shift</kbd> of the previous and next year (the last day of the month if the day does not exist).
-   <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> selects the day and closes the dialog.
-   <kbd>Escape</kbd> closes the dialog. The focus goes back to the input.

A key move to a day outside of `minDate` and `maxDate` does not move the focus.
The standalone `DatePicker` has the same calendar keyboard. It does not move the focus on mount.
